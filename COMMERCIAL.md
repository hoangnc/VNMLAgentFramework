## FAQ

**Q: I'm building a side project. Do I need a license?**

A: No, as long as it's non-commercial. If it starts generating revenue, you need a license.

**Q: I'm a freelancer building apps for clients. Do I need a license?**

A: Depends. If the client uses it in production commercially, yes. If it's a prototype, no.

**Q: Can I use it in my open-source project?**

A: Yes, if your project is non-commercial. If your project is commercial, no.

**Q: What happens after 4 years?**

A: The license automatically converts to Apache 2.0. You can then use it freely for any purpose.

**Q: Can I get a custom license?**

A: Yes. Contact us for OEM, white-label, or enterprise custom terms.

**Q: What if I violate the license?**

A: You must either purchase a commercial license or stop using VNML in production.

## Why BSL?

We chose BSL 1.1 because it balances:

- **Community access** — free for individuals and non-commercial use
- **Sustainability** — commercial users support ongoing development
- **Long-term openness** — auto-converts to Apache 2.0 after 4 years

This model is used by MariaDB, HashiCorp (Terraform, Vault), and Redis.

Our goal: keep VNML free for individuals while building a sustainable business that supports long-term development.
